Relationship to pathologic response category

RCB class and pathologic response category are related but not interchangeable. In the original cohort, minimal residual disease (RCB-I, 17% of patients) carried the same prognosis as pathologic complete response, and RCB-I can be reported alongside pCR in a synoptic without contradicting either.

RCB-0
No residual invasive disease in breast or nodes. This is not identical to pCR: pCR is defined by AJCC as ypT0/Tis ypN0, and RCB-0 permits residual in situ disease in the breast. Report pCR from the ypTNM stage, not from the RCB class.
RCB-I
Minimal residual burden. Prognostically equivalent to pCR in the original data.
RCB-II
Moderate residual burden. Corresponds loosely to a partial response.
RCB-III
Extensive residual burden. Corresponds loosely to an incomplete or no response, but is defined by tumor burden rather than by the absence of response.

RCB measures the volume of what remains. Pathologic response category (complete, partial, incomplete, no response) measures how much the tumor changed. A case can be RCB-III by burden yet still show a partial response, so the two should be reported from their own data rather than inferred from each other. RCB complements ypTNM and does not replace it.

Registry coding (NAACCR EOD / SEER*RSA)

Residual Cancer Burden is a site-specific data item under the Breast schema only (NAACCR item 1178, effective for diagnosis year 2026 and later). It has no cross-site equivalent, so this calculator is not validated for rectal, gastrointestinal, gynecologic, or any other tumor site.

Coded range
0.000 – 9.999, three decimal places
Rounding
Record to the nearest one thousandth; all three digits beyond the decimal point must be filled in. A report stating 3.151 is coded 3.151; a report stating 3 is coded 3.000.

X.999
Post-neoadjuvant surgery completed but the RCB score is not documented in the CAP protocol or synoptic pathology report, or the surgical pathology report is unavailable.
Blank
Must be blank if the diagnosis year is before 2026.

A score is only derived when neoadjuvant therapy and a surgical resection have both been performed, and only from the values on the CAP protocol or synoptic pathology report.

Scope

RCB measures residual disease in the breast and regional lymph nodes after neoadjuvant therapy and resection. It is the most validated measure of residual disease for breast cancer and is prognostic across subtypes, treatments, and stage groups. The same arithmetic applied to another tumor site would produce a number, but no validated cut-points exist for those sites, so the class assigned here would not be interpretable.

Formula: RCB = 1.4(finv · dprim)0.17 + [4(1 − 0.75LN) · dmet]0.17, where dprim = √(d1 × d2), finv = (1 − %in situ/100) × cellularity/100, LN = positive nodes, dmet = largest nodal metastasis (mm). This is an independent implementation for education and verification. Confirm results against the official MD Anderson calculator before using them in a clinical report.
